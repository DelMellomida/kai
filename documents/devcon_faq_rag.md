**Q: What is DEVCON Philippines?**
A: DEVCON (Developers Connect), registered as DevConnect Philippines Inc., is a non-profit and the largest volunteer community of software developers and tech future makers in the Philippines. Its vision is a technology-empowered Philippines for all, and its mission is to build vibrant developer communities empowered with knowledge, open-source software, and opportunities to solve impactful national problems.

section: About DEVCON

---
**Q: When and how did DEVCON start?**
A: DEVCON began in 2009 as a project of the Philippine Software Industry Association (PSIA), piloting with DEVCON Visayas at the TechBar in Cebu City. It grew into a ten-city Next Wave Cities tour, partnered with the Philippine Society of IT Educators (PSITE) to reach students, and later registered with the SEC as a non-profit.

**Q: What is Kai?**
A: Kai is a robot assembled by DEVCON Manila chapter volunteers and built by the DEVCON Jumpstart Cohort 4 AI Engineering Interns.

---
id: kai-how
section: Kai (robot)
synonyms: [how Kai works, Jetson, face tracking, edge computing, on-device]
---
**Q: How does Kai work?**
A: Kai runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano behind a cardboard face, processing all AI workloads on-device with no cloud round trips. Its camera runs MediaPipe FaceMesh at 320x240 for face tracking; camera feed, computer-vision inference, and mirroring motor responses all run locally in real time — an example of edge computing. Its neck servo pans up to 180 degrees.

---
id: kai-hardware
section: Kai (robot)
synonyms: [what powers Kai, offline, NVIDIA Ampere, CUDA, TensorRT, barangay]
---
**Q: What powers Kai?**
A: Kai is built on NVIDIA Ampere architecture with Tensor Cores, using the CUDA ecosystem and TensorRT for near-zero-latency neural network execution. It runs fully offline, so it works anywhere — from a smart city to a far-flung barangay with no stable internet.

---

section: Kai (robot)
synonyms: [Kai tech stack, tools behind Kai, NMBLR.AI]
---
**Q: What's the tech stack behind Kai?**
A: Kai's stack includes Claude Code, Micro:bit, Qwen, Google AI Suite, and NVIDIA. The Jetson Orin Nano was lent by the NMBLR.AI team.

**Q: What is the DEVCON Jumpstart Internship?**
A: The DEVCON Jumpstart Internship is an internship with the Philippines' largest tech non-profit, working on national-scale, mission-driven projects using a modern stack of Claude Code, Google AI, and Sui Smart Contracts, alongside industry AI practitioners. It puts top students on an AI Engineering track mentored by working engineers. Cohort 4 is currently running with members from Manila, Iloilo, and CDO in a flexible hybrid setup, and past cohorts have come out of Iloilo and CDO.

**Q: What is DEVCON's overall stance on AI?**
A: DEVCON views AI as the biggest opportunity Filipino developers have had in a generation — not something to fear or wait out. Its POV: don't just consume AI, build with it. DEVCON believes the nation that learns fastest and builds boldest wins the next decade.

**Q: Is DEVCON worried AI will replace developers?**
A: No. DEVCON believes AI changes what a developer's job looks like, not whether developers are needed. The winners will be those who orchestrate AI rather than resist it, and DEVCON's job is to make sure every Filipino geek is in that group.

**Q: Why does DEVCON think the Philippines is well-positioned for AI?**
A: DEVCON points to three things stacked together that are rare in the region: decades of service-industry DNA from BPO, 700,000+ STEM graduates a year, and a culture of Bayanihan — collective effort as a competitive advantage. DEVCON argues that combination is exactly what the AI era rewards.

**Q: What does "Engineering an AI-Ready Nation" mean?**
A: To DEVCON it means readiness is not a slogan but concrete action — chapters, trainings, internships, and tools reaching real developers in real towns, not just a keynote in a Manila ballroom. It frames the effort as nation-building, one chapter at a time.

**Q: Does DEVCON think the Philippines is behind in AI?**
A: DEVCON reframes it: the gap is the opportunity. Roughly 85% of Philippine businesses have not adopted AI yet, and DEVCON sees each of those gaps as a company waiting for a founder — a runway, not a deficit.
